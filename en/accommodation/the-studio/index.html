The Studio

A naturist B&B in Carvoeiro, in the Algarve. Adults only — never more than four guests in the whole house.

The Studio has a sitting area and a real kitchenette: sink, hob, microwave, fridge, coffee machine, pots and tableware — you can genuinely cook. Its terrace opens onto the pool — five metres, and you're in the water.

30 m², all on one level — plus the terrace.

Inside: a double bed and a bathroom of your own. There is also fibre broadband with Wi-Fi and a television. The air conditioning provides heating too, keeping the studio cool in summer and warm at the start and end of the season. Housekeeping every day, except Sunday.

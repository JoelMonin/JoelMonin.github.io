The Room

A naturist B&B in Carvoeiro, in the Algarve. Adults only — never more than four guests in the whole house.

The Room gives you everything you need — and is just as peaceful as the rest of the house. Its terrace is yours, and it too opens onto the pool, some ten metres away.

27 m², all on one level — plus the terrace.

Inside: a double bed, a bathroom, a fridge, a kettle and tableware — enough for a simple meal in your room or on your terrace, without a kitchen. There is also fibre broadband with Wi-Fi and a television. The air conditioning provides heating too, keeping the room cool in summer and warm in April and October. Housekeeping every day, except Sunday.

Naturism at Casa Somba

Here, we live naked — it is part of life in this house. No one is checking up on you: people cover up when it's cool, when they go out, or when they don't feel ready yet. If you come as a couple and only one of you is a naturist, you are both very welcome: the other can wear swimwear throughout the stay if they prefer. All we ask is that at least one of you is a naturist — so that nobody here feels observed.

In practice: around the pool and in the garden, we're naked. On your terrace and in your own place, you do as you wish. At breakfast, everyone comes as they are — naked, in a sarong, dressed: each at their own pace.

Coming alone?

Welcome. A house with at most four guests may be the easiest possible place to do it: no established group to fit into, no organised activities, nobody to keep up with.

Never practised naturism?

In a house with at most four guests you never have to face a crowd: you go down to the pool when you feel like it — in an hour, or tomorrow morning. And people feel at ease far sooner than they expect.

We do not accommodate children.

Casa Somba is a peaceful home, run by a couple who live here.

Naturism here is simply part of the holiday: swimming, reading, napping in the shade, slipping back into the water after a walk, and meeting at breakfast or by the pool.
